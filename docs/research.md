# OpenBOM target research

Checked 12 October 2026. Family: product-lifecycle, not previously built in the registry. Scope: engineering revision release and change control, distinct from job-shop production, product information merchandising and quality inspection.

## Bill and selection

US$21,600/year is a modelled 20-editor Company seat bill at US$90 per editor per month billed annually. It is not a customer invoice. Data-record and CAD charges are additional. No confirmed OpenBOM buyer or invoice was found in accessible Command Centre customer notes. Buyer fit stays 2, not a claimed customer relationship. Live CRM access was not used.

Scores in order: annual bill, data-shaped, buyer fit, search demand, one-session buildable, import path.

Search for OpenBOM alternatives returned comparison directories and owner discussions. This supports alternative intent, not quantified search volume. The low buyer-fit score reflects the missing NZ/AU invoice. This is the best researched candidate in the available unbuilt family, not a claim to have scored every SaaS market.
